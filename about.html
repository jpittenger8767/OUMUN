About Us

Who we are

We are a student organization at the University of Oklahoma dedicated to providing a platform for students to engage with the topic of global affairs and develop their leadership skills, along with fostering a community of informed and active citizens.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to advancing the understanding of the United Nations and global affairs through simulations, cultural events, and conferences. We emphasize the importance of cooperative problem-solving, and prepare citizens to engage with the global community.

What We Do

Here at OU Model UN, we organize and host various different simulations, cultural events, and conferences to provide our members with opportunities to engage with global issues and develop their leadership skills. We strive to create an inclusive environment where students can cooperate with each other and learn what it means to be a global citizen.

When We Meet

We meet every Monday from 7:00 PM to 8:00 PM in Farzaneh Hall, Room 148 on the OU campus.

Spreading awareness of global affairs, one student at a time.
